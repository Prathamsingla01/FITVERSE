Understanding Macronutrients

🥚 Protein

Calories per gram: 4

Daily intake: 0.8-1g per pound of body weight

Best sources:

  • Chicken breast - 31g per 100g
  • Eggs - 6g per egg
  • Fish - 25-30g per 100g
  • Greek yogurt - 10g per 100g
  • Cottage cheese - 11g per 100g
  • Lean beef - 26g per 100g

Benefits: Muscle repair, recovery, satiety

🌾 Carbohydrates

Calories per gram: 4

Daily intake: 2-3g per pound of body weight

Best sources:

  • Brown rice - 23g per 100g
  • Oats - 27g per 100g
  • Sweet potatoes - 20g per 100g
  • Whole wheat bread - 41g per 100g
  • Bananas - 23g per 100g
  • Quinoa - 21g per 100g

Benefits: Energy, performance, recovery

🥑 Fats

Calories per gram: 9

Daily intake: 0.3-0.5g per pound of body weight

Best sources:

  • Olive oil - 14g per tablespoon
  • Almonds - 14g per ounce
  • Avocado - 10g per 100g
  • Salmon - 13g per 100g
  • Peanut butter - 8g per tablespoon
  • Coconut oil - 14g per tablespoon

Benefits: Hormone production, nutrient absorption

💡 Macro Calculation Tips

Step 1: Determine your maintenance calories

Step 2: Adjust based on your goal (+/- 300-500 kcal)

Step 3: Calculate macros:

  • Protein: 1-1.2g per lb of body weight
  • Fats: 0.3-0.4g per lb of body weight
  • Carbs: Fill remaining calories

Essential Micronutrients

Vitamins

  • Vitamin D: Bone health, immune function
  • Vitamin C: Immune support, collagen synthesis
  • B Vitamins: Energy metabolism, muscle function
  • Vitamin A: Vision, cell growth
  • Vitamin E: Antioxidant, muscle recovery

Minerals

  • Iron: Oxygen transport, energy production
  • Magnesium: Muscle function, recovery
  • Zinc: Immune function, protein synthesis
  • Calcium: Bone health, muscle contraction
  • Potassium: Electrolyte balance, muscle function

Hydration

  • Daily intake: 3-4 liters minimum
  • During workout: 500ml per 30 minutes
  • Post-workout: 150% of fluid lost
  • Electrolytes: Sodium, potassium, magnesium
  • Timing: Consistent throughout the day

Meal Preparation Tips

Planning

  • Plan meals for the entire week
  • Create a shopping list
  • Choose recipes with common ingredients
  • Batch cook similar items
  • Invest in quality containers

Preparation

  • Cook proteins in bulk
  • Prepare grains and rice
  • Chop vegetables in advance
  • Use slow cooker for convenience
  • Label containers with dates

Storage

  • Store in airtight containers
  • Refrigerate for 3-4 days
  • Freeze for longer storage
  • Keep sauces separate
  • Maintain proper temperature

Tracking

  • Use food scale for accuracy
  • Log meals in an app
  • Track macros daily
  • Adjust based on results
  • Review weekly progress
