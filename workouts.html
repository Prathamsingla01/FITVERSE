Weekly Workout Routines

Monday - Chest & Triceps

Focus: Upper body pushing movements

Duration: 60 minutes

Difficulty: Intermediate

  • Barbell Bench Press - 4x6-8
  • Incline Dumbbell Press - 3x8-10
  • Cable Flyes - 3x10-12
  • Tricep Dips - 3x8-10
  • Rope Tricep Pushdown - 3x12-15
  • Overhead Tricep Extension - 3x10-12

Tuesday - Back & Biceps

Focus: Upper body pulling movements

Duration: 60 minutes

Difficulty: Intermediate

  • Deadlifts - 4x4-6
  • Barbell Rows - 4x6-8
  • Lat Pulldowns - 3x8-10
  • Barbell Curls - 3x8-10
  • Dumbbell Curls - 3x10-12
  • Hammer Curls - 3x10-12

Wednesday - Legs & Abs

Focus: Lower body strength and core

Duration: 70 minutes

Difficulty: Advanced

  • Barbell Squats - 4x6-8
  • Leg Press - 3x8-10
  • Leg Curls - 3x10-12
  • Leg Extensions - 3x10-12
  • Calf Raises - 3x15-20
  • Weighted Crunches - 3x12-15

Thursday - Shoulders & Arms

Focus: Deltoids and arm isolation

Duration: 55 minutes

Difficulty: Intermediate

  • Barbell Shoulder Press - 4x6-8
  • Lateral Raises - 3x12-15
  • Reverse Flyes - 3x12-15
  • Dumbbell Curls - 3x10-12
  • Tricep Dips - 3x8-10
  • Face Pulls - 3x15-20

Friday - Full Body

Focus: Compound movements

Duration: 65 minutes

Difficulty: Intermediate

  • Squats - 3x8-10
  • Bench Press - 3x8-10
  • Barbell Rows - 3x8-10
  • Overhead Press - 3x8-10
  • Deadlifts - 2x5-6
  • Pull-ups - 3x6-10

Saturday - Cardio & Core

Focus: Cardiovascular health and core strength

Duration: 45 minutes

Difficulty: Beginner

  • Treadmill Running - 20 minutes
  • Planks - 3x60 seconds
  • Mountain Climbers - 3x20 reps
  • Russian Twists - 3x20 reps
  • Leg Raises - 3x15 reps
  • Bicycle Crunches - 3x20 reps

Yearly Progression Plan

Months 1-3: Foundation

Build foundational strength and perfect form.

  • Focus on compound movements
  • 3 days per week training
  • Moderate weights with high reps
  • Learn proper technique
  • Build consistent habit

Months 4-6: Strength

Increase strength and muscle mass.

  • 4-5 days per week training
  • Progressive overload focus
  • Heavier weights, lower reps
  • Add isolation exercises
  • Track all workouts

Months 7-9: Hypertrophy

Build muscle size and definition.

  • 5-6 days per week training
  • Moderate weights, 8-12 reps
  • Increased volume
  • Advanced techniques
  • Nutrition optimization

Months 10-12: Peak

Achieve peak performance and conditioning.

  • Periodized training cycles
  • Deload weeks included
  • Advanced programming
  • Competition preparation
  • Maintenance focus
